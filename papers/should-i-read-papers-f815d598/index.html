Should I read papers?

  • Authors:
  • Michael Robert Bernstein
Read the paper
Published: October 21, 2014

📜 Abstract

My answer to the question “Should I read papers?” is a resounding yes, but really what I mean is: you should stretch. Give yourself a challenge. There’s more beneath the surface than you could ever imagine.

✨ Summary

This HTML article is an editorial rather than a conventional research paper. Bernstein argues that software developers and other practitioners should engage with research papers, but that “reading” need not mean reading every word from beginning to end. He presents skimming references, investigating authors, and understanding a paper’s domain from its abstract as legitimate forms of engagement. The central argument is that papers should be approached as continuing relationships with ideas and their authors, and that readers should not be intimidated by unfamiliar material.
